# CloudEagle.ai

> CloudEagle.ai is an AI-powered SaaS Management and Identity Governance platform that helps IT, Security, Finance, and Procurement teams discover, govern, optimize, and procure their SaaS and AI applications from a single unified platform. Backed by Y Combinator, recognized in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for SaaS Management Platforms and the 2026 Gartner® Market Guide for Software Asset Management Tools, and rated 4.7 on G2. CloudEagle connects to 500+ apps out of the box — delivering feature-level usage insights, automated access governance, and AI-powered contract intelligence. Customers typically save 10–30% on SaaS spend within days of onboarding. CloudEagle is SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS, and SOX compliant.


## Product Hubs

- [SaaS Management](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/product/saas-management): Achieve 100% visibility into your SaaS stack. Detect shadow IT, eliminate duplicate and unused apps, automate license reclamation, and replace spreadsheets with accurate real-time license and contract tracking. Customers save 10–30% on software spend.
- [SaaS Procurement](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/product/saas-procurement): Manage the full procurement lifecycle from intake to renewal. Access the world's largest SaaS benchmarking database and buying guides, automate approval workflows via Slack or Jira, and negotiate confidently with peer pricing intelligence.
- [SaaS Security & Compliance](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/product/saas-security-and-compliance): Enforce least-privilege access across every SaaS app. Continuously monitor privileged users, automate user access reviews, detect shadow IT and unauthorized app usage, and generate SOC 2–ready audit logs automatically.
- [Identity Governance](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/product/identity-governance): Automate the full employee access lifecycle across SCIM and non-SCIM apps. Provision birthright access on day one, enable self-service app requests via Slack, and instantly revoke all access at offboarding with a complete audit trail.
- [AI Governance](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/product/ai-governance): Continuously discover AI tools and agents as they appear in your environment. Block unauthorized AI access in real time, track Copilot and AI app ROI, and give security and leadership teams board-level visibility into AI usage and data exposure.

- [Excessive Privileged Users](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/saas-security-compliance/excessive-privileged-users): Identify users with admin or elevated permissions that exceed their role requirements. Continuously monitor privilege levels across all connected apps and right-size access automatically.

Replace manual spreadsheet-based reviews with AI-assisted workflows that cut review time by 80% and generate audit-ready evidence.

## Identity Governance — Sub-pages

- [Self-Service App Catalog](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/identity-governance/self-service-app-catalog): Give employees a Slack-native app catalog to request access to approved tools instantly — without opening an IT ticket. IT teams define policies; employees self-serve within guardrails.
- [Employee Onboarding & Offboarding](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/identity-governance/jml-onboarding-offboarding): Automate joiner, mover, and leaver workflows across all SaaS apps. New hires get role-based birthright access the moment HR updates their status; departing employees are fully deprovisioned instantly.
- [Automate App Access Requests](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/identity-governance/automate-app-access-requests): Replace email chains and IT tickets with structured, automated app access request workflows. Route approvals to the right managers, enforce time-bound access, and log every decision for compliance.
